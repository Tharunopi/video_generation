 Uncle Bhoopi and the X-traordinary Letter X 
Uncle Bhoopi (whispering like an explorer): 
Psst… hello, my clever explorers! 
Today’s letter is X — the exciting, mysterious letter that loves to cross paths! 
The sound of X is /ks/ — like in box, fox, and mix! 
Let’s try it together: /ks/… /ks/… X! 
Excellent! 
It was a mysterious morning in Alphabet Town. 
I put on my explorer vest, packed my magnifying glass, and sang:

 “X is for x-ray, xylophone, and spot, 
X is the letter that’s full of a lot!”

I followed my treasure map to the park, where a big red X was painted on the ground. 
“Look, friends!” I said. “X marks the spot! That means something exciting is hiding!” 
I dug and dug… until — clink! 
Out popped a shiny Xylophone! 
“X is for Xylophone!” I said, tapping it with a stick. 
Ting! Tong! Ting! The music made everyone dance. 
Just then, Xavier the Fox ran up, wearing his superhero cape. 
“Hi Uncle Bhoopi!” he said. 
“Hi Xavier! You’re just in time — X is in your name!” 
He grinned. “Let’s go on an X adventure!” 
We zoomed to the Alphabet Museum, where a skeleton stood tall behind glass. 
“Whoa!” I said. “X is for X-ray! It helps doctors see our bones!” 
We looked at our own X-ray pictures — our bones looked so funny! 
Then I grabbed a big chalk stick and drew a huge X on the sidewalk. 
“See this? X is made of two lines that cross in the middle — like friends giving a big high-five!” 
“Let’s draw it together — /ks/… /ks/… X!” 
Next, we spotted a Pirate Flag flapping in the breeze. 
“X marks the treasure!” I shouted. 
“Let’s find it!” said Xavier. 
We followed the map, found another red X, and dug up — a box of golden chocolate coins! 
“Treasure!” we cheered.
